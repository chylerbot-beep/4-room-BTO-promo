The practical questions.

Does $35,888 cover the whole renovation?
It covers the complete package scope listed above. Personalised features—such as feature walls, false ceilings, kitchen partitions or additional carpentry—are quoted separately and agreed before work starts.
Can I visit a completed project before signing?
Yes. We encourage it. A site visit lets you experience the carpentry, finishing and spatial decisions in person. WhatsApp us and we will arrange a suitable completed-home visit when one is available.
When should I contact Woodates?
As soon as you have an estimated key-collection date. A typical 5-room BTO renovation takes approximately 10–14 weeks, while planning and design happen before physical work begins.
What should I prepare for the planning call?
  • Your HDB floor plan
  • Estimated key-collection date
  • Two or three reference images you genuinely like
  • Any non-negotiable storage or lifestyle needs
Do you renovate other property types?
Yes. We also work on 3-room and 4-room BTOs, resale HDB homes and condominiums. These are quoted based on their individual scope.
